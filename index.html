From steel to a finished edge

A Royal nail clipper passes through about eighty processes. Heat, quench, and finishing are what make the cut last.

  1. 01 layers

    Clipper steel

    We start with S15CM heat-treatment steel made by POSCO, specified for nail clippers.

  2. 02 local_fire_department

    900°C, then oil

    Blanks go through a 900°C furnace and an oil quench so the edge stays sharp and durable.

  3. 03 precision_manufacturing

    Eighty processes

    Automatic lines carry each piece through forming, grinding, and finishing before it leaves the factory.

  4. 04 content_cut

    Built in-house

    The same workshop develops our nose-hair trimmers, not only clippers bought in and rebranded.
